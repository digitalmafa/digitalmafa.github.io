Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

Welcome to DigitalMAFA. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you visit our website or contact us about our digital marketing services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website or submit a contact form, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if provided
  • The service you are interested in
  • Your message and project requirements

Basic technical information, such as browser type, device information, and website usage data, may also be collected if analytics or similar tools are enabled on our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to your questions and inquiries.
  • Provide information about our services and pricing.
  • Understand your project requirements.
  • Improve our website and user experience.
  • Protect our website against spam, fraud, or misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Contact Forms and Third-Party Services

Our contact form may use FormSubmit or other third-party services to deliver your inquiry to our email inbox. When you submit a form, the information you provide may be processed by that service according to its own privacy policy and terms.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party providers. Please review their policies before submitting information if you have concerns.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, understand website usage, or improve performance. Third-party tools, if enabled, may also use these technologies.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some website features may not work as intended if certain cookies are disabled.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

We may use Google AdSense and other third-party advertising services to display advertisements on our website in the future.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to collect information about visitors' interactions with this website and to serve, measure, and improve advertisements.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites, subject to applicable settings and policies.

Users may manage personalized advertising preferences through Google's Ads Settings. For more information about how Google uses data on partner websites, please visit:
"How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

We will update this Privacy Policy if our advertising practices change. Please note that third-party advertising services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable requirements.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers when necessary to operate our website, deliver messages, provide services, or comply with applicable laws and valid legal requests.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information submitted through our website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, maintain relevant business records, provide services, or meet legal requirements. Retention periods may vary depending on the purpose and circumstances.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to raise concerns about how it is handled.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address listed below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

Our website is intended for a general business audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review the matter.

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before sharing information.
